# The Principles of Diffusion Models

Chieh-Hsin Lai · Yang Song · Dongjun Kim · Yuki Mitsufuji · Stefano Ermon

<a id="source"></a>

This unofficial web edition is based on *The Principles of Diffusion Models*, available as [arXiv:2510.21890v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21890v3).

The English web edition currently includes **Chapters 1–12**, covering deep generative modeling, the variational and score-based perspectives, the continuous-time Score SDE framework, flow-based models from normalizing flows to flow matching, a unified view of diffusion objectives and parameterizations, connections with optimal transport and Schrödinger bridges, guidance for controllable generation and preference alignment, numerical solvers for fast diffusion sampling, distillation, fast generators trained from scratch, and probability evolution on discrete spaces. These chapters retain the original English text, mathematical notation, figures, tables, algorithms, and references.

## Reading the web edition

Use the chapter navigation to move between sections and the page contents to find a topic within a section. The search field covers the pages available in this edition. On smaller screens, open the navigation menu; wide equations can be scrolled horizontally.

Original equation and figure numbers are retained. The [book contents](en/book-outline.md) show which chapters are available. Links to material that is not yet available open a page with its current status.

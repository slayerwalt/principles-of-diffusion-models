# The Principles of Diffusion Models

Chieh-Hsin Lai · Yang Song · Dongjun Kim · Yuki Mitsufuji · Stefano Ermon

This is an unofficial web edition of *The Principles of Diffusion Models*, based on [arXiv:2510.21890v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21890v3). The project is converting the original English text into pages that can be read, searched, and maintained by chapter.

The current sample is **Chapter 1, Deep Generative Modeling**. It introduces the mathematical setting, prominent model families, and the taxonomy used throughout the book. The sample retains the source text, mathematical notation, figures, and references; it is undergoing conversion review.

[Begin Chapter 1](en/chapters/01-deep-generative-modeling/index.md) · [View the full book contents](en/book-outline.md)

## Reading the web edition

Use the chapter navigation to move between sections and the page contents to find a topic within a section. The search field covers the pages available in this edition. On smaller screens, open the navigation menu; wide equations can be scrolled horizontally.

Original equation and figure numbers are retained so the sample can be checked against the paper. References to material not yet converted point to the original PDF where possible. The [book contents](en/book-outline.md) show which chapters are available and which are awaiting conversion.

## English first, Chinese later

The English edition will be converted and reviewed before Chinese translation begins. The [Chinese translation page](zh-CN/index.md) records this plan; it does not yet contain translated chapters.

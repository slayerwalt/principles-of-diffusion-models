# The Principles of Diffusion Models

Chieh-Hsin Lai · Yang Song · Dongjun Kim · Yuki Mitsufuji · Stefano Ermon

<a id="source"></a>

This unofficial web edition is based on *The Principles of Diffusion Models*, available as [arXiv:2510.21890v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21890v3).

The English web edition currently includes **Chapters 1–4**, covering deep generative modeling, the variational and score-based perspectives, and the continuous-time Score SDE framework. These chapters retain the original English text, mathematical notation, figures, tables, algorithms, and references.

## Reading the web edition

Use the chapter navigation to move between sections and the page contents to find a topic within a section. The search field covers the pages available in this edition. On smaller screens, open the navigation menu; wide equations can be scrolled horizontally.

Original equation and figure numbers are retained. The [book contents](en/book-outline.md) show which chapters are available. Links to chapters that are not yet available open a page with their current status.

## English first, Chinese later

The English edition will be converted and reviewed before Chinese translation begins. The [Chinese translation page](zh-CN/index.md) records this plan; it does not yet contain translated chapters.

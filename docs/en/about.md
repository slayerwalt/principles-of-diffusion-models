# Source and attribution

*The Principles of Diffusion Models* is written by **Chieh-Hsin Lai, Yang Song, Dongjun Kim, Yuki Mitsufuji, and Stefano Ermon**. This unofficial web edition uses the authors' English LaTeX source for **arXiv:2510.21890v3** as its conversion baseline.

- [Original publication record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21890v3)
- [Original PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.21890v3)
- [Original LaTeX source](https://arxiv.org/src/2510.21890v3)
- [Web edition repository](https://github.com/slayerwalt/principles-of-diffusion-models)

## Conversion notes

The web edition splits the source into chapter and section pages. It preserves the English wording, original numbering, and mathematical notation while changing the presentation to Markdown and browser-rendered mathematics. Figures are presented as image assets; citations and internal references are converted into web links.

Chapter 1 is the initial sample and remains under review. Material awaiting conversion is listed in the [book contents](book-outline.md). If the web edition and the source differ, consult the original PDF and report the affected passage through the repository.

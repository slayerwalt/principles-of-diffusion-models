# 8.3 Classifier-Free Guidance {#sec-cfg}

## 8.3.1 Foundation of Classifier-Free Guidance {#foundation-of-classifier-free-guidance}

<span id="fig-cfg"></span>

![Classifier-free guidance combines conditional and unconditional score estimates](../../assets/chapter-08/classifier-free-guidance.png)

**Figure 8.2:** **Illustration of CFG.** The guided direction is a linear combination of the conditional score $\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t|{\mathbf{c}})$ and the unconditional score $\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t)$, controlled by $\omega$. For $0\leq\omega\leq1$, this interpolates between the two directions; for $\omega>1$, it extrapolates beyond the conditional score in the conditional direction. The resulting field is used to steer the sampling trajectory toward the conditioning signal.

Source: Created by the authors.

*Classifier-free guidance* (CFG) ([Ho and Salimans, 2021](references.md#ref-ho2021classifier)) removes the need for a separately trained classifier by expressing the classifier-guidance direction through conditional and unconditional scores. Bayes’ rule gives

<span id="eq-cfg-bayes-direction"></span>

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{c}}|{\mathbf{x}}_t)
=
\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t|{\mathbf{c}})
-
\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8.3.1}
$$

Substituting this identity into [Equation (8.2.3)](02-classifier-guidance.md#eq-cg-omega) gives the oracle CFG field

<span id="eq-cfg-model"></span>

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\mathbf{s}}^{\mathrm{CFG}}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,{\mathbf{c}};\omega)
&:=
\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t)
+
\omega
\left(
\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t|{\mathbf{c}})
-
\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t)
\right)
\\
&=
\omega
\underbrace{\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t|{\mathbf{c}})}_{\text{conditional score}}
+
(1-\omega)
\underbrace{\nabla_{{\mathbf{x}}_t}\log p_t({\mathbf{x}}_t)}_{\text{unconditional score}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8.3.2}
$$

With exact scores, [Equation (8.3.2)](03-classifier-free-guidance.md#eq-cfg-model) is algebraically the same guided field as [Equation (8.2.3)](02-classifier-guidance.md#eq-cg-omega). At $\omega=1$, it reduces to the exact conditional score. For other guidance strengths, its fixed-time tilted-density interpretation should be distinguished from the marginals generated by the guided dynamics, as discussed in [Section 8.4.2](04-training-free-guidance.md#subsec-guided-marginal-path).

The hyperparameter $\omega$ again plays a critical role in controlling the influence of the conditioning information (we take $\omega \ge 0$):

-   At $\omega = 0$, the model behaves as an unconditional diffusion model, completely ignoring the conditioning.

-   At $\omega = 1$, the model uses the conditional score without additional guidance.

-   For $\omega > 1$, the model places more emphasis on the conditional score and less on the unconditional score, strengthening alignment with ${\mathbf{c}}$ but typically reducing diversity.

## 8.3.2 Training and Sampling of CFG {#training-and-sampling-of-cfg}

**Joint Training of Unconditional and Conditional Diffusion Models via CFG.** CFG requires a diffusion model trained to support both conditional and unconditional predictions. Rather than training two separate models, CFG uses a single model ${\mathbf{s}}_{\boldsymbol{\phi}}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,{\mathbf{c}})$ and randomly replaces the conditioning input by a null token $\emptyset$ during training:

-   with the null input, the model learns the unconditional prediction ${\mathbf{s}}_{\boldsymbol{\phi}}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,\emptyset)$;

-   with the original condition, it learns the conditional prediction ${\mathbf{s}}_{\boldsymbol{\phi}}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,{\mathbf{c}})$.

The null condition is used with probability $p_\mathrm{uncond}$, a user-specified condition-dropout probability. The full training procedure is shown in [Algorithm 4](03-classifier-free-guidance.md#alg-cfg-training), alongside standard unconditional training in [Algorithm 3](03-classifier-free-guidance.md#alg-no-cfg-training). The guidance weight $\omega$ is introduced only at sampling time and is not part of this training objective.

**Conditioned Sampling with CFG.**

Once the model ${\mathbf{s}}_{\boldsymbol{\phi}^\times}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,{\mathbf{c}})$ is trained using [Algorithm 4](03-classifier-free-guidance.md#alg-cfg-training), CFG forms the learned guided field

<span id="eq-cfg-model-nn"></span>

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\mathbf{s}}_{\mathrm{model}}^{\mathrm{CFG}}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,{\mathbf{c}};\omega)
&:=
\omega
\underbrace{{\mathbf{s}}_{\boldsymbol{\phi}^\times}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,{\mathbf{c}})}_{\text{conditional score estimate}}
+
(1-\omega)
\underbrace{{\mathbf{s}}_{\boldsymbol{\phi}^\times}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,\emptyset)}_{\text{unconditional score estimate}}
\\
&\approx
{\mathbf{s}}^{\mathrm{CFG}}({\mathbf{x}}_t,t,{\mathbf{c}};\omega).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8.3.3}
$$

During sampling, the unconditional score estimate in the reverse-time SDE or PF-ODE is replaced by ${\mathbf{s}}_{\mathrm{model}}^{\mathrm{CFG}}$. Thus CFG defines a modified sampling field by combining the learned conditional and unconditional score estimates.

This formulation enables controllable generation by adjusting $\omega$, providing a simple post-training trade-off between conditioning strength and sample diversity without requiring a separately trained classifier or unconditional diffusion model.
